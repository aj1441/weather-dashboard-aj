Tab Descriptions
Weather Tab:

City/State input, unit toggle, theme controls, current weather, 7-day forecast, save/view city buttons.
Saved Cities Tab:

List of saved cities, quick load buttons, remove city option.
History Tab:

Table or list of previous weather lookups, date/time, city, weather summary.
About Tab:

App info, credits, version, maybe a link to your repo.
